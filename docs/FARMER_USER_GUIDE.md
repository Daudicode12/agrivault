# 🌾 Farmer's Guide: Market Dashboard

## How to Check Market Prices

### Step 1: Login
1. Open AgroVault app
2. Click "Sign In"
3. Enter your email and password

### Step 2: Go to Market Dashboard
1. Click "Market Dashboard" in the menu
2. You'll see the price analysis page

### Step 3: Enter Your Crop Name
1. **Select Commodity**: Choose your crop from the dropdown
   - Maize
   - Beans
   - Wheat
   - Rice
   - Sorghum
   - Irish Potatoes
   - Coffee

2. **Select Your County**: Choose where you want to sell
   - All Counties (see prices everywhere)
   - Nairobi
   - Kiambu
   - Nakuru
   - Mombasa
   - Kisumu
   - Uasin Gishu
   - Machakos

3. **Select Time Period**: How far back to look
   - 30 days (1 month)
   - 90 days (3 months) - Recommended
   - 180 days (6 months)
   - 365 days (1 year)

### Step 4: View Results

You'll see:

#### 📊 Price Summary
- **Current Price**: Today's price in your county
- **Average Price**: Average over the selected period
- **Price Range**: Lowest and highest prices

#### 💡 Recommendation
The system will tell you:
- **SELL** - Sell your crop now
- **HOLD** - Wait for better prices
- **CONSIDER SELLING** - Prices are okay, your choice

Plus:
- **Urgency**: How urgent (high/moderate/low)
- **Why**: Detailed explanation

#### 📈 Price Graph
- See how prices changed over time
- Spot trends (going up or down)
- Hover over the line to see exact prices

#### 📅 Market Analysis
- **Trend**: Are prices rising or falling?
- **Forecast**: What will happen in the next 30 days?
- **Seasonal**: When are the best months to sell?

## Example: Farmer John's Story

**John grows Maize in Kiambu County**

1. He opens Market Dashboard
2. Selects:
   - Commodity: **Maize**
   - County: **Kiambu**
   - Period: **90 days**

3. He sees:
   - Current Price: **KES 3,850 per bag**
   - Average: **KES 3,750**
   - Trend: **Rising** (+5.2%)
   - Forecast: **Will rise 4.2% in 30 days**
   - Seasonal: **Prices peak in May (3 months away)**
   - Recommendation: **HOLD**

4. The system explains:
   - "Prices are rising"
   - "Forecast shows increase"
   - "May is the best month to sell"

5. John decides to **wait 3 months**

6. In May, he sells at **KES 4,200 per bag**

7. **Result**: He earned **KES 350 more per bag** by waiting!

## Understanding Recommendations

### SELL (Red)
- Prices are high now
- Likely to drop soon
- Good time to sell

### HOLD (Green)
- Prices are low now
- Expected to rise
- Wait for better prices

### CONSIDER SELLING (Yellow)
- Prices are okay
- Not urgent
- Your choice

---

## When to Sell

### Best Times:
- When recommendation says "SELL"
- During peak months (shown in seasonal section)
- When prices are above average
- When trend is "falling" (sell before it drops more)

### Best to Wait:
- When recommendation says "HOLD"
- During valley months
- When prices are below average
- When trend is "rising" (wait for higher prices)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I know which county to choose?
**A:** Choose your county to see local prices. Or select "All Counties" to see the overall market.

### Q: What if my crop isn't listed?
**A:** Currently we support 7 major crops. More will be added soon.

### Q: How accurate are the forecasts?
**A:** Forecasts are based on historical data and seasonal patterns. They're usually reliable but not guaranteed.

### Q: Should I always follow the recommendation?
**A:** The recommendation is advice based on data. Consider your own situation (storage capacity, urgent cash needs, etc.)

### Q: What if I need to sell urgently?
**A:** If you need cash urgently, sell even if the recommendation says "HOLD". Your needs come first.

### Q: How often is the data updated?
**A:** Market data is updated daily. Analysis runs every 4 hours.
